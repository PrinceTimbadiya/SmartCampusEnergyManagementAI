1. RAW DATASET
   1.1 Dataset Overview
   The raw dataset contains 500 records of electricity consumption data collected from four campus buildings over approximately 4 months (May - August 2026).

Source: Manual meter readings and observational data collected during campus sustainability audit.

Time Period: May 1, 2026 to August 16, 2026 (108 days)

Buildings Covered:

Academic Block

Library

Canteen

Hostel

Columns:

Date (daily readings)

Building (categorical)

Electricity_Consumption_kWh (numerical)

Occupancy_Count (numerical)

Average_Temperature_C (numerical)

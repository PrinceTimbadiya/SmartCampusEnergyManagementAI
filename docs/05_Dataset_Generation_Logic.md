Variation Factors:

Day of Week: Higher consumption on weekdays (Mon-Fri), lower on weekends

Temperature Effect: +2-5 kWh per degree Celsius increase above 34°C

Occupancy Effect: Higher occupancy = higher consumption

Special Events: Peak days (events, exams) show spikes

Seasonal Trend: Gradual increase from May to August (summer)
